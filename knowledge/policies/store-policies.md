# Online Shop Customer Support FAQ

## 1. Orders
- **Q1: Do I need an account to place an order?**
  - **Answer**: No. You can check out as a guest. Creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, and speed up future checkouts.
- **Q2: How do I place an order?**
  - **Answer**: Add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, enter shipping details, choose a payment method, and confirm your order. You will receive an email confirmation once it is placed.
- **Q3: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
  - **Answer**: If your order has not been packed yet, we may be able to change or cancel it. Contact support as soon as possible with your order number.
- **Q4: I did not receive an order confirmation email. What should I do?**
  - **Answer**: Check your spam/junk folder and verify the email address used at checkout. If it is still missing after 30 minutes, contact support and we will resend it.
- **Q5: Why was my order canceled?**
  - **Answer**: Orders can be canceled due to payment authorization issues, stock availability, or automated fraud checks. If this happens, you will not be charged (or you will be refunded automatically).

## 2. Shipping & Delivery
- **Q6: Where do you ship?**
  - **Answer**: We ship to most countries/regions listed at checkout. If your address is not available, it means we currently cannot ship there.
- **Q7: How much does shipping cost?**
  - **Answer**: Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and delivery speed. Promotions like free shipping (if offered) will be shown automatically.
- **Q8: How long does delivery take?**
  - **Answer**: Estimated delivery times are shown at checkout and in your shipping confirmation email. Processing typically takes 1–2 business days before dispatch.
- **Q9: How do I track my order?**
  - **Answer**: Once your order ships, we will email a tracking link. If you have an account, you can also find tracking under My Orders.
- **Q10: My package is late, missing, or marked delivered but I cannot find it.**
  - **Answer**: First, check tracking updates, your mailbox/neighbor, and any safe-place notes from the carrier. If it still has not turned up after 24 hours (marked delivered) or is delayed beyond the last estimate, contact support and we will investigate.

## 3. Returns & Refunds
- **Q11: What is your return policy?**
  - **Answer**: You can return most items within 30 days of delivery as long as they are unused and in original packaging (unless the item arrived defective).
- **Q12: How do I start a return?**
  - **Answer**: Contact support with your order number and the items you want to return. We will send return instructions and, where applicable, a return label.
- **Q13: Who pays for return shipping?**
  - **Answer**: If the return is due to damage, defect, or our error, we cover return shipping. For "changed my mind" returns, return shipping may be deducted from your refund where allowed.
- **Q14: When will I receive my refund?**
  - **Answer**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method after we receive and inspect the return. This typically takes 3–10 business days, depending on your bank/provider.
- **Q15: Can I exchange an item?**
  - **Answer**: We usually do not do direct exchanges. The fastest option is to return the original item (if eligible) and place a new order.
- **Q16: What if my item arrived damaged or defective?**
  - **Answer**: Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item, packaging, and shipping label. We will arrange a replacement or refund.
- **Q17: Are any items non-returnable?**
  - **Answer**: Some items may be non-returnable for hygiene, safety, customization, or regulatory reasons. If so, it will be clearly stated on the product page and/or at checkout.

## 4. Payments & Promotions
- **Q18: What payment methods do you accept?**
  - **Answer**: We accept major credit/debit cards and other local methods shown at checkout. Available options can vary by country.
- **Q19: When will I be charged?**
  - **Answer**: You are charged when your order is placed (or when payment is authorized, depending on the method). If an item ships separately, some providers may show multiple authorizations.
- **Q20: Why was my payment declined?**
  - **Answer**: Common reasons include incorrect billing details, insufficient funds, bank security checks, or limits on international/online purchases. Try again, use a different method, or contact your bank.
- **Q21: How do I use a discount or promo code?**
  - **Answer**: Enter the code at checkout in the promo/discount field and apply it before paying. Only one code may be used unless stated otherwise.
- **Q22: Can I get an invoice/receipt?**
  - **Answer**: A receipt is emailed after purchase. If you need an invoice with company details (e.g., VAT), contact support with your order number and billing information.

## 5. Products & Stock
- **Q23: Is the item I want in stock?**
  - **Answer**: If you can add it to your cart, it is generally in stock. If it sells out, the product page will show "Out of stock."
- **Q24: Will you restock out-of-stock items?**
  - **Answer**: Some items are seasonal or limited. If restocking is planned, you may see a "Notify me" option on the product page.
- **Q25: Do product photos match the real item?**
  - **Answer**: We aim for accurate images and descriptions, but colors can vary by screen settings and lighting. Check the product details for material and sizing notes.

## 6. Account & Support
- **Q26: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
  - **Answer**: Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. You will receive a reset email if the address matches an account.
- **Q27: How do I update my address or email?**
  - **Answer**: Sign in and go to Account Settings to update your details. If an order is already placed, contact support quickly to request changes.
- **Q28: How do I delete my account?**
  - **Answer**: Contact support from the email linked to your account. We will verify your request and process deletion in line with legal/recordkeeping requirements.
- **Q29: How can I contact customer support?**
  - **Answer**: Use the help/contact form on our website or reply to any order confirmation email. Include your order number for faster assistance. Alternatively, reach our support phone line at 1-800-555-SHOP (Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm EST).
- **Q30: What are your support hours and response times?**
  - **Answer**: Support is available Monday–Friday (excluding holidays), 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST. We typically respond to emails/tickets within 1–2 business days; urgent shipping/return issues are prioritized.

## 7. Privacy
- **Q31: How do you use my personal data?**
  - **Answer**: We use your data to process orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.
- **Q32: Can I request access or deletion of my data?**
  - **Answer**: Yes. Contact support with your request. We will handle it according to applicable privacy laws and may need to verify your identity.
